Surrounded by a bunch of creative designers, marketing wizards and production architects who are hungry to do perfect work that the world has not seen. The competitive edge at Alter Ego is the diverse and collective experience of the people at work. It has a potent mix of creative and business-oriented mind sets. Here there is a perfect ratio of the right brain and left-brain centric people. We ensure that the spotlight of the market is on your product and services.

Our team consists of 4 divisions :

  1. Marketing and sales : Our dedicated marketing team helps to find a suitable solution for client’s exhibition requirements pertaining to different market segments. It starts with a detailed analysis of client’s needs in a particular exhibition and act as a bridge between our design team and client to come up with a suitable design solution. Our team provides comprehensive assistance to client throughout exhibition. This division heading by sales director, which consists lead generation executives, business development and client servicing managers.
  2. Design Studio : Our in-house designers provide a complete rendering of the stand space in three dimensions with different viewing angles for the client to understand all subtle aspects in the stand design. It includes graphics, display dimensions, branding as well as overall design language, which reflects client’s positioning in respective market segments. A detailed working drawing with prototypes will be provided to client for approval before we start production at workshop level. Leading by a design head, which Comprising 3D, Graphic designers and an AutoCAD designer for technical specification and risk assessment.
  3. Production : An art of converting DESIGN to FORM happens at workshop level. We have a blend of expertise, experience, and enthusiasm, with dedicated factory manager, project managers and site supervisors along with skilled labourers. We use latest project management software to get complete control over all processes which ensure timely reports, updates and zero error on each project.
  4. Administration : This division handles day to day activities & accounting to ensure smooth administration and pre and post sales activities for a better project completion.
